Facility move planning

Plan what moves, where it goes, what fits, and what happens first.

01

Inventory

Item groups, quantities, size and weight ranges, handling needs, and source context.

02

Decisions

Assign each item to the destination, active use, storage, sale or donation, transfer, disposal, or specialist review.

03

Destination layout

Editable room geometry, proposed placements, route checks, and linked spatial views.

04

Storage

Unit fit, aisle clearance, rack and stack rules, retrieval access, and load order.

05

Move plan

Estimate inputs, ordered work, open checks, labels, and a revision-linked ZIP.

03 / DATA AND USAGE

Review images before analysis.

Check each photo for faces, screens, badges, and documents before analysis; a local privacy editor can permanently cover any region. MoveLens re-encodes selected images locally to remove embedded metadata and original filenames, then keeps the prepared copies in your browser.
